Darrell Seale

Air Force Veteran & International Executive

Darrell Seale is a United States Air Force veteran and retired international business executive based in Abu Dhabi. Across two decades in aerospace and defense, the founding of a veterans’ nonprofit, and travel to 142 countries, his work has centered on leadership, service, and community.

About Darrell

A Career Built on Service, at Home and Abroad

Darrell Ray Seale began his professional life as a commissioned officer in the United States Air Force, earning the Meritorious Service Medal and the Air Force Commendation Medal among other decorations. The discipline and accountability of military service carried into a senior management career in aerospace and defense, which in 2014 brought him to Abu Dhabi with Lockheed Martin.

Service has never been confined to his day job. Seale co-founded Patriot Divers, a nonprofit that uses scuba diving to support wounded and disabled veterans, and he has certified more than 300 divers as an instructor since 1999. In Abu Dhabi, he competed on a Unified Sports volleyball team at the 2019 Special Olympics World Games and presented medals to more than 50 athletes.

Now retired, Darrell remains active in the Abu Dhabi community he calls home. His background as a veteran and executive is set out in full on the About page, and an account of his 25 years as a scuba instructor traces the teaching career he began in 1999.
